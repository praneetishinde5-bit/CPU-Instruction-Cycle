Fetch in detail

MAR ← PCthe address of the next instruction goes into MAR
MDR ← M[MAR]memory is read and the instruction arrives in MDR
IR ← MDRthe instruction is copied into IR
PC ← PC + 1the PC moves on to the next instruction

PC (Program Counter) remembers which instruction comes next. IR (Instruction Register) holds the instruction being worked on. ACC (Accumulator) is the CPU's working number. MAR (Memory Address Register) holds the address to read or write. MDR (Memory Data Register) holds the data going to or from memory. The program and its numbers share one memory, so each row has its own address.
